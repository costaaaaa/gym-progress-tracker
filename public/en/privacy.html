4. Body measurements and consent

Weight and body measurements are considered health data. We process them only after you have given us a consent that is separate from the terms of use, and we use them only to show you your charts and trends over time. They are not visible to other users.

Apple Health and Health Connect. In the iPhone and Android app you can connect LiftIndex to Apple Health (iPhone) or to Health Connect (Android). It is optional: you turn it on yourself from Profile → Settings, choose between Read only and Read and write (you can change this whenever you like) and decide on the phone's permissions screen which data to give access to. This use has been covered by the body-measurements consent since version 2026-10 of 2 October 2026: for this reason we asked for it again, even from those who had already given it.

  • What we read: weight, body fat percentage and waist circumference (the latter only on iPhone). For each day we save a single value, the last one recorded, not the individual samples nor the device that measured them.
  • What we write, only with Read and write: the measurements you enter in LiftIndex and the workouts completed in Focus mode (type, start and end). They stay on your phone, in the Health app or in Health Connect, and you manage them from there.
  • What we do not do: we do not read other Health data (for example heart rate, sleep or steps), we do not use it for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone else. Apple and Google receive no data from us.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect at any time from Profile → Settings and choose whether to also delete the imported values; the ones you entered yourself remain. You can also remove the permissions from the phone's settings.

You can withdraw your consent at any time from Profile → Settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out and results in the deletion of all your body measurements: before you confirm we offer you the chance to download them. If in the future you want to use this feature again we will ask you for a new consent.

5. Groups

You can create a group or join someone else's with an invite code. Before you join we show you what the other members will see and ask you to accept it.

  • What the other members see: your username, level, experience points, streak of consecutive weeks, number of workouts and volume lifted (kg) for the current week, your role in the group and the date you joined.
  • What they do not see: email, date of birth, weight and body measurements, and the details of your workouts (exercises, weights, reps, notes).
  • The username is the same one you use to sign in: choose a strong password. Repeated login attempts are blocked anyway.
  • Leaving: you can leave a group at any time (from the group or from Profile → Settings). Your data disappears from the leaderboard immediately. Whoever created the group can remove you or delete the group.
  • Age: groups are available only from age 16.
  • Reports: if a group's name or a member's username seems unlawful to you or contrary to the terms, you can report it from the group (Report content). The report is read only by whoever runs LiftIndex, not by the other members. We send you a receipt and then the outcome by email. If we change a name we tell whoever created the group or the user concerned, with the reasons, without saying who reported.

6. AI Coach

The AI Coach is an optional chat, not yet available to everyone, that answers questions about your workouts. The answers are generated automatically by an artificial intelligence model: they may contain errors and are not medical advice. You need a confirmed email to use it.

  • Consent: we ask for it the first time you use it, separately from the others. You can withdraw it at any time from the AI Coach panel: withdrawal immediately deletes all your chats and the AI Coach query log.
  • What the model sees: your question, the latest messages of the same chat and the data needed to answer, read from your account: username, date of birth, sex, plans, workouts, weights, notes, experience points and achievements. Body measurements only if you have also given the consent of point 4. Never email, password or other users' data. It sees the breakdown of sets by muscle group only with Plus or Pro; with Free it sees the weekly total.
  • Provider: requests are processed by Mistral AI SAS (France), on servers in the European Union, as data processor. It does not use your data to train its own models and does not keep it after generating the answer.
  • Saved chats: we keep your chats on our server so that you can resume them. You can delete one or all of them whenever you want from the chat list; in any case each one is deleted 30 days after its last message.
  • AI Coach query log: to answer you, the AI Coach makes requests to your data, called queries (they are not the questions you write). For each query we record the text, the outcome, how much data it read and the date; we do not record the data read. We need it to check that the AI Coach reads only your data. The basis is your consent to the AI Coach, as for chats. We delete each entry 30 days after its date, and the whole log when you withdraw consent or delete your account. Deleting chats does not delete the log, which is included in the export of your data.
  • No automated decisions: the AI's answers have no effect on your account and are not used to profile you.

7. Connected AI assistants

You can connect an external AI assistant to your account, so that it reads some of your data and answers your questions about your workouts. This is different from the AI Coach of point 6: here you choose the assistant, with its safeguards and not those of the AI Coach. You must be at least 18 years old, calculated from the date of birth you declared, have confirmed your email and have given the "connected AI assistants" consent. We may turn this feature off at any time.

To connect an assistant you paste our server's address into its settings; the list of apps you can connect is on the AI apps you can connect page. A consent page on liftindex.app then opens, showing:

  • the assistant's name and domain;
  • the assistant's profile: recipient and country, one for people living in the European Economic Area and one for people living outside it, the legal basis of each one, the link to its privacy policy and the date the profile was last checked;
  • an unticked box, "Also share body measurements", which you can turn on only if you have already given the consent of point 4;
  • a request to confirm your password, unless you have already confirmed it for another connection in the last 2 hours;
  • the Allow and Deny buttons.

We ask for the consent to processing the first time and it remains separate from the others; the choice about body measurements, instead, applies to each individual connection. At every new connection we send you an email with the assistant's name: if it was not you, you can close it from your account page, which is linked in the email.

What the assistant can read, read-only: it cannot modify or delete anything.

  • the list of exercises: the catalog plus the ones you created yourself (name and muscle group);
  • the trend of the weights of one or more exercises searched by name, even partially: the maximum weight per day, over the period the assistant asks for, up to one year;
  • workouts per week: number of sessions, volume and working sets (by muscle group with Plus or Pro, as a total with Free), over the period the assistant asks for, up to one year;
  • a summary: total number of workouts and sets, average weight and total volume;
  • workout sessions over the period the assistant asks for, even spanning several years: date, number of exercises and sets;
  • the detail of a session: the exercises in the order you did them and, for each set, weight, reps, intensity technique and whether it is a warm-up;
  • the records of each exercise: maximum weight, estimated one-rep max and maximum bodyweight reps, with the date;
  • plans: the list (name, creation date and which one is active) and the content of a plan, that is days, exercises, rest and planned sets;
  • only if you ticked the box for that connection and as long as the consent of point 4 remains active, body measurements (date, weight, body fat and muscle percentage, chest, arm, waist and leg circumferences): the most recent one and the history over the period the assistant asks for, even spanning several years.

It does not read email, password, date of birth, the notes of workouts and plans, the descriptions of plans, the AI Coach chats, groups or other users' data. The questions you ask the assistant and its answers do not pass through us. Its answers are generated by the provider: they may contain errors and are not medical advice.

The provider of the assistant you connect is an independent controller of the data it receives, not our processor: we have no contract with it. From that moment its terms and its privacy policy apply, including on retention, deletion and model training: we do not control them. Deleting data from LiftIndex does not delete it at the provider.

Country and transfers.

  • If you live in the European Economic Area: each assistant's profile indicates a recipient based in the European Economic Area (the provider's European company). On our side there is no transfer outside the European Economic Area; the provider may then process the data outside it as well, for example in the United States, with its own safeguards and under its own responsibility.
  • If you live outside the European Economic Area: the recipient may also be based outside it. If there is a European Commission adequacy decision for that recipient (Art. 45 GDPR), that is the basis. Otherwise the transfer is based on your explicit consent (Art. 49.1.a GDPR), given on the connection page after we have informed you of the risks: in the recipient's country your data may have lower protections, for example narrower rights or no independent supervisory authority.
  • Checking the profiles: we re-check them at least every 3 months; if an assistant's recipient, country or legal basis changes, we close its connections and email you to reconnect it.
  • Assistants not on the list: they cannot connect, except those on your own computer (below). If we remove an app from the list, we close its connections and notify you by email.
  • Assistants on your own computer: they can connect even if they are not on the list, and the consent page points this out: in that case the data arrives on your computer and from there you decide where it goes, with no provider named by us.

You will find the details for each app on the AI apps you can connect page.

Read log. For each read we record:

  • the assistant, the type of read and the parameters (for example the exercise name and the period);
  • the date and time, the outcome and the size of the response;
  • never the content of the response nor the question you asked.

The basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): for security, to prevent abuse and to let you see who read what. We keep it for 90 days, even after you close the connection, and it is included in the export of your data together with the list of connections. There are limits on the number of reads: beyond the limit we refuse them for a while.

Closing connections. You can close one or all connections from Profile → Settings, Connected AI assistants section on the website:

  • "Withdraw consent" closes all connections and also withdraws the consent;
  • changing or recovering your password closes all connections;
  • 90 days without use close the individual connection;
  • we also close a connection if its assistant's profile changes or if we remove its app from the list (see above).

Closed connections are deleted by the nightly cleanup; the read log remains for the 90 days mentioned above. Deleting your account deletes everything: connections, log and consent.

Reads have no effect on your account and are not used to profile you.

8. Who we share data with

  • Hosting provider: Aruba S.p.A. (Italy), which hosts the server where the data is stored, as data processor.
  • Backups: they are kept on the same Aruba server. If we add a copy with another provider we will say so here.
  • Service emails (for example the link to reset your password, the code to confirm your email, the warnings before an unconfirmed account is deleted and the notice to the old address when you change email): they are sent through the email service of Aruba S.p.A., the same data processor that hosts the server. We do not send newsletters or promotional emails. Service emails are plain text only, with no images or pixels that record whether you open them.
  • AI Coach: Mistral AI SAS (France), as data processor, only if you use the AI Coach and only for what is stated in point 6.
  • Connected AI assistants: only if you connect one, the assistant's provider is an independent controller of the data it receives (see point 7).
  • Other users: no other user sees your data, except the members of the groups you choose to join, and only for what is stated in point 5.
  • Authorities: only if the law requires us to (for example by order of a judge) and only the data requested.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. The data we process ourselves stays within the European Economic Area; the data you let a connected AI assistant read goes to the provider of the assistant you choose (see point 7).

9. How long we keep data

  • Account data (including account type), profile and workouts: as long as you keep your account. When you delete it (Profile → Settings) we delete all the linked data, except reports (see below). We reserve the right to delete accounts that have been inactive for more than 24 months, notifying you first by email.
  • Body measurements: as long as your consent remains valid. If you withdraw it they are deleted.
  • Group membership: as long as you stay in the group. When you leave, are removed or the group is deleted, we delete it immediately.
  • Reports: while they are open and then for 5 years from the decision, so that we can respond to disputes or requests from the authorities; then we delete them. If the reporter or the person reported deletes their account, the report remains without the link to the account (the reported name, the reason and the decision remain).
  • AI Coach chats: each one up to 30 days after its last message, or until you delete it or withdraw consent.
  • AI Coach query log: each entry up to 30 days after its date, or until you withdraw consent (see point 6).
  • Connections with AI assistants and read log: connections until they are closed and then until the nightly cleanup, which deletes them (see point 7); the read log for 90 days (even after the connection is closed).
  • Accounts never confirmed: if the email is never confirmed, the account is deleted with all its data no earlier than 37 days after sign-up, at the first useful nightly cleanup; before that we send two warning emails, on day 30 and on day 35, and we keep the date of the last warning until the deletion or the confirmation.
  • Email confirmation codes: valid for 30 minutes, stored only as a hash; the nightly cleanup deletes those that expired more than a week ago.
  • Use of the sign-up code: as long as you keep your account. If you delete your account after the code was applied, the use of the code stays (which code, its state and dates) without your account: it is needed to count the code's places. If the code was not applied, the use is deleted with the account.
  • Phone access codes and password recovery links: they expire on their own (30 days of inactivity and 1 hour) and are cleaned up periodically.
  • Login attempts (IP address): a few minutes or hours, the time needed to limit abuse.
  • Web server logs (access and errors, with the IP address and the page requested): 14 days, then they are deleted by automatic rotation.
  • Technical service logs (mainly errors, which may contain the IP address and the page requested): they overwrite themselves when they reach a maximum size.
  • Usage statistics (Umami): 13 months, then they are deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in backups for up to 14 days, then it is removed by the automatic rotation of the backups.

10. Your rights

You can at any time exercise the rights provided for by Articles 15-22 of the GDPR:

  • access your data and obtain a copy of it;
  • rectify inaccurate data (from your profile);
  • have it deleted: you can delete your account from Profile → Settings;
  • receive your data in a structured, readable format ("Export your data" button in your profile), including the state of your email and the use of the sign-up code;
  • ask us to restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent, without this affecting the rest of the service.

To do so, use the profile features or write to info@liftindex.app. We reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

These links prepare the email with the subject already written:

  • data access;
  • deletion;
  • objection;
  • withdrawal of consent.

Deleting your LiftIndex account

You can delete your LiftIndex account and all the linked data at any time, from the website or the app:

  1. sign in at liftindex.app or in the LiftIndex app;
  2. go to Profile → Settings, on the website or in the app;
  3. choose "Delete account" and confirm.

If you cannot sign in, write to info@liftindex.app from the account's email address: we delete it within one month. The following are deleted: account, profile, workouts, body measurements, group membership, AI Coach chats and query log, connections with AI assistants and their log, email confirmation codes, and consents. If the sign-up code was applied, its use stays without your account, to count the code's places; if it was not applied, the use is deleted. They remain only in backups, for up to 14 days.

11. Minimum age

To sign up you must be at least 14 years old. If you are under 14 you cannot create an account: if we find out that an account belongs to someone under that age, we delete it. To create or join a group you must be at least 16. To connect an AI assistant you must be at least 18.

12. Cookies and similar tools

We use a single cookie, a technical session cookie: it keeps you signed in. It is created only when you sign in and lasts until you close the browser, or 30 days if at login you choose "Stay signed in for 30 days". We do not use profiling cookies or third-party tools that track your browsing; the fonts are hosted on our own servers.

The site also saves some information in your browser's local memory (local storage), which stays on the device until you sign out or delete it from the browser settings:

  • that you have signed in, and your identifier and username, to show you the pages without reloading them every time; they are deleted when you sign out;
  • the light or dark theme you chose;
  • the draft of the workout in progress in Focus, so you do not lose it if you close the page;
  • that you closed the notice about the app in body measurements, so we do not show it to you again.

To show you the right link to the app (App Store or Play Store), the page detects in your browser whether you are using an iPhone, an iPad or an Android phone. This information stays on the device: we do not receive it and we do not store it.

These are all tools necessary to let you use the service as you requested and they do not require consent.

13. Security

Passwords are stored with a one-way encryption algorithm, connections are protected by HTTPS, access to the servers is restricted and we make regular backups. No system is perfectly secure: in the event of a data breach that presents a risk to you we will notify you as required by law.

14. Changes to this policy

We will tell you about important changes by email and with a notice, shown once, the first time you next sign in on the site or in the app. We also update this page and the version shown at the top. If the changes concern the consent to body measurements, to the AI Coach or to connected AI assistants, we will ask for your consent again.

Terms of use

Version 2026-11 · updated on 17 November 2026 (email confirmation and sign-up codes)

These terms govern the use of LiftIndex, the website (liftindex.app) and mobile app for tracking workouts and gym progress ("the service"). By signing up you declare that you have read and accepted them. How we process your personal data is described in the privacy policy.

1. Who we are

The service is provided by Andrea Costamagna, an individual. Contact: info@liftindex.app.

2. Your account

  • To use the service you must be at least 14 years old and provide truthful information, in particular your date of birth.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for everything that happens with your account. If you think someone has had access to it, change your password right away and write to us.
  • An account is personal: do not share it, and do not create more than one to get around limits or blocks.
  • The account works right away, but within 7 days you must confirm your email with the 6-digit code we send you when you sign up. The code is valid for 30 minutes: if it expires you can ask for another one. After the 7 days, until you confirm your email, you can only confirm it, correct the address, export your data, delete the account and sign out.
  • An account whose email is never confirmed is deleted, with all its data, workouts included, no earlier than 37 days after sign-up. Before that we send you two warning emails, on day 30 and on day 35.
  • You can change your email address, by confirming your password. If your email is already confirmed, the new address takes effect only after you enter the code sent to that address, and at that moment we send a notice of the change to the old address.

3. What the service does

LiftIndex lets you create plans, log your workouts, see statistics and progress, and earn points and achievements. Some features may change, be added or be removed over time. There are three options: Free, Plus and Pro. Plus and Pro can't be bought today and are assigned only by the owner of the service, including through a sign-up code. Free has limits, described on the Free, Plus and Pro page. If you move down from Plus or Pro, your data is not deleted: workout plans that go over the new limits stay usable for training but are read-only until you are back within the limits. A sign-up code gives you the Plus or Pro option for the stated length of time, or with no end date, counted from the day you confirm your email: until then the account stays as it is. The places for each code are limited and your place is held for the 7 days you have to confirm your email. If the owner turns the code off before you confirm, you do not get the option; if you confirm after the 7 days, you get it only if the code has not expired and a place is still free, otherwise the account stays as it is. A code never moves your account to a lower option than the one it has, and never shortens it. When that period ends, the account goes back to Free, with the rules above. Codes cannot be transferred or exchanged for money. No payment is taken without your explicit choice and, if payments are introduced, these conditions will be updated first.

4. No medical advice

LiftIndex is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses or treatment. Statistics, recovery times and suggestions are general guidance based on the data you enter. Before starting or changing a training program, especially if you have a medical condition or an injury, are pregnant or take medication, consult a doctor. You train at your own risk and must stop if you feel pain or discomfort.

If you do not follow these rules we may suspend or close your account, notifying you where possible.

6. Your content

The data you enter (workouts, plans, notes, measurements) remains yours. You authorize us to store and process it only to provide you with the service, as described in the privacy policy. You can download or delete it at any time from your profile. A connected AI assistant reads it only if you connect it yourself, with the separate consent described in the privacy policy.

7. Groups and reports

A group's name and your username are visible to the other members. They must not be unlawful, offensive or discriminatory, pretend to be another person, or contain other people's personal data or advertising.

  • How to report: if a member of a group you are in, or the group's name, breaks these rules or is unlawful, use Report content on the group's page. Explain the reason and confirm that the report is made in good faith.
  • How we decide: every report is reviewed by a person, not by an automated system, with diligence and impartially. We may reject it, uphold it or change the reported name.
  • If we change a group's name: whoever manages the group cannot edit it for 14 days. If we change it a second time, the name can no longer be edited: to ask for a change, write to us.
  • If we change your username: from then on you sign in with the new name; your password does not change.
  • Outcome and reasons: we tell the person who reported the outcome by email. We tell whoever manages the group, or the user whose name we change, the decision and the reasons, without saying who reported.
  • If you disagree: write to us at info@liftindex.app and we will review the decision. You can always go to court.
  • Abuse: repeated and clearly unfounded reports are a misuse of the service (point 5).

8. Connected AI assistants

If you are at least 18 years old, you can connect an external AI assistant to your account so that it reads some of your data, read-only. You need a confirmed email to connect an assistant. You choose which assistant to connect and you accept its terms: we are not a party to the relationship between you and its provider. The assistant's answers are generated by that provider: they may contain errors and are not medical advice (see point 4).

Reads beyond the service's usage limits are refused for a while. We may close a connection or remove an app from the list of those you can connect for reasons of security, abuse, or if its provider's details change. You can close a connection whenever you like. You will find all the details in the privacy policy.

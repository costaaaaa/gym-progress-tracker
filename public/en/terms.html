3. What the service does

LiftIndex lets you create plans, log your workouts, see statistics and progress, and earn points and achievements. Some features may change, be added or be removed over time. At the moment the service is free. If we introduce paid features we will tell you in advance and these conditions will be updated: no payment is taken without your explicit choice.

4. No medical advice

LiftIndex is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses or treatment. Statistics, recovery times and suggestions are general guidance based on the data you enter. Before starting or changing a training program, especially if you have a medical condition or an injury, are pregnant or take medication, consult a doctor. You train at your own risk and must stop if you feel pain or discomfort.

If you do not follow these rules we may suspend or close your account, notifying you where possible.

The data you enter (workouts, plans, notes, measurements) remains yours. You authorize us to store and process it only to provide you with the service, as described in the privacy policy. You can download or delete it at any time from your profile. A connected AI assistant reads it only if you connect it yourself, with the separate consent described in the privacy policy.

7. Groups and reports

A group's name and your username are visible to the other members. They must not be unlawful, offensive or discriminatory, pretend to be another person, or contain other people's personal data or advertising.

  • How to report: if a member of a group you are in, or the group's name, breaks these rules or is unlawful, use Report content on the group's page. Explain the reason and confirm that the report is made in good faith.
  • How we decide: every report is reviewed by a person, not by an automated system, with diligence and impartially. We may reject it, uphold it or change the reported name.
  • If we change a group's name: whoever manages the group cannot edit it for 14 days. If we change it a second time, the name can no longer be edited: to ask for a change, write to us.
  • If we change your username: from then on you sign in with the new name; your password does not change.
  • Outcome and reasons: we tell the person who reported the outcome by email. We tell whoever manages the group, or the user whose name we change, the decision and the reasons, without saying who reported.
  • If you disagree: write to us at info@liftindex.app and we will review the decision. You can always go to court.
  • Abuse: repeated and clearly unfounded reports are a misuse of the service (point 5).
